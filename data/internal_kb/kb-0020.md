# Break-glass admin access { #break-glass-admin-access }

## Overview { #overview }

This runbook defines the operational procedure for utilizing break-glass credentials within the Acme Payments infrastructure. These accounts are intended exclusively for emergency scenarios where standard identity provider access is unavailable or compromised. This protocol is owned and maintained by the security-team and remains in active status.

## Access Lifecycle and Expiration { #access-lifecycle-and-expiration }

When a break-glass credential is provisioned, it is subject to strict temporal constraints to minimize the blast radius of elevated privileges. All break-glass admin accounts expire automatically after 4 hours. Once the expiration threshold is reached, the credential is programmatically invalidated, and all active sessions associated with that principal are terminated. Engineers must ensure all necessary remediation steps are completed within this window. If further time is required, a new request must be submitted and authorized through the standard emergency access workflow.

## Audit and Review Process { #audit-and-review-process }

Transparency and accountability are critical components of our security posture. To maintain compliance and verify the legitimacy of emergency access, every break-glass session is reviewed by two members of the security team. This post-incident review occurs within 24 hours of the session termination. The reviewers are tasked with verifying that the actions taken during the session align with the stated emergency requirements and checking for any unauthorized configuration changes or data access. 

All logs generated during a break-glass session are ingested into our centralized SIEM for immutable retention. Reviewers must document their findings and sign off on the session audit trail to close the incident ticket. Failure to complete this review process will trigger an automated escalation to the security engineering lead.
