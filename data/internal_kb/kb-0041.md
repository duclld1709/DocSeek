# Engineering onboarding checklist { #engineering-onboarding-checklist }

This document outlines the mandatory onboarding sequence for all new engineers joining the platform-team. Adherence to these protocols ensures consistency in our deployment environments and maintains our operational security standards. 

## Environment access and security { #environment-access-and-security }

Access to our internal systems is granted on a tiered basis to minimize risk. All new hires are provided with local development environments and staging access upon arrival. However, production read access is strictly restricted until the engineer completes the initial 30-day onboarding period. During this time, engineers should focus on familiarizing themselves with our FastAPI service architecture and the CI/CD pipelines managed by the platform-team. Access requests submitted before the 30-day threshold will be automatically rejected by our IAM provider.

## On-call rotation and training { #on-call-rotation-and-training }

Our incident response strategy relies on the collective expertise of the engineering department. To maintain service reliability, all engineers are required to join the on-call rotation after completing their probationary training. We enforce a mandatory shadowing period for new staff to ensure safe operational practices. Your first on-call shift must be shadowed by a senior engineer who will oversee incident triage and resolution steps. This shadowing session is non-negotiable and must be scheduled at least one week prior to the start of the assigned shift. 

## Engineering standards { #engineering-standards }

All code contributions must adhere to our internal style guide, which favors modular, testable Python code. New engineers are expected to complete their first pull request within the first two weeks of their tenure. This task is intended to verify environment configuration rather than test feature implementation. Reach out to the platform-team lead if you encounter blockers regarding environment authentication or repository permissions.
