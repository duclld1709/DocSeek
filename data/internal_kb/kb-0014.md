# On-call escalation policy { #on-call-escalation-policy }

This document outlines the standard escalation procedures for the sre-team to ensure reliable incident response. All on-call rotations are managed via our centralized alerting platform, which integrates directly with our FastAPI-based infrastructure monitoring services.

## Escalation workflow { #escalation-workflow }

When an alert triggers an incident, the system notifies the primary on-call engineer. If the primary on-call engineer does not acknowledge a page within 5 minutes, the system automatically escalates the alert and pages the secondary on-call engineer. This automated transition ensures that no alert remains unaddressed due to connectivity issues or hardware failure at the primary responder's location.

## SEV1 response requirements { #sev1-response-requirements }

For incidents classified as SEV1, the urgency necessitates an expanded response team. In addition to the standard primary and secondary escalation chain, SEV1 incidents also page the engineering manager on duty immediately. This is designed to facilitate rapid resource allocation and cross-departmental communication for critical system degradation or total service outages. Engineers are expected to follow the incident response protocol once a SEV1 is declared, ensuring all logs and diagnostic data are captured in the relevant ticket for post-incident review.

## Responsibilities { #responsibilities }

It is the responsibility of the on-call engineer to ensure their contact information is accurate and that they have reliable network access during their shift. If an engineer anticipates being unavailable, they must coordinate a shift handoff with another team member and update the on-call schedule in the internal dashboard. Failure to acknowledge pages within the specified 5-minute window will trigger the escalation, and repeated instances may require a review of the on-call rotation effectiveness.
