Ledger database connection
pool sizing
This runbook outlines the conﬁguration
constraints and operational parameters for the
ledger-service database connection pool.
Proper sizing is critical to maintaining database
stability and preventing connection exhaustion in
the PostgreSQL cluster managed by the
platform-team.
Pool capacity constraints
To ensure predictable resource utilization across
the Kubernetes cluster, each ledger-service
pod is strictly limited in its connection footprint.
Each pod maintains a maximum of 50 database
connections at any given time. Engineers should
account for this limit when scaling the number of
replicas or when modifying the ledger-service
concurrency models. Exceeding this per-pod
limit will trigger connection queuing at the driver
level, leading to increased latency for transaction
processing.
Lifecycle and idle management
Eﬃcient resource reclamation is handled by the
connection pooler integrated into the ledger-
service runtime. To mitigate the accumulation of
orphaned or stale sessions, the pool policy
enforces a strict timeout mechanism.
Connections that remain idle for more than 300
seconds are automatically closed and removed
from the pool.
This threshold balances the overhead of frequent
TCP handshakes against the necessity of
keeping the database connection count within
operational limits. If services experience high
latency during traﬃc spikes, verify that the
connection pool is not prematurely closing active
connections due to misconﬁgured keep-alives or
aggressive application-side timeouts. Direct any
questions regarding database connectivity tuning
to the platform-team internal Slack channel.
